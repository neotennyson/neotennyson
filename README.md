## AI trading desk
Six systems built in six weeks with five different AI models (~95k lines). Each runs its own strategy; they review each other's ideas and learn from failures. Nothing trades real money until it survives real costs.

- **Nemesis** — a falsification engine: tries to kill strategy ideas before they cost a rupee. 800+ tests, real broker cost model, live options-chain recorder.
- **Ploutos** — pre-registered "alpha hunts" on index options and futures, with a paper-trading shadow runtime.
- **NIAN** — volatility-aware index-futures experiments.
- **Quasar** — multi-index trend ensemble with risk-based position sizing.
- **Predator** — event-day options strategy with a built-in risk governor.
- **TiltGuard** — a trader-discipline layer: loss caps and cooldowns.
