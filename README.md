## AI trading desk
Six systems built in six weeks with five different AI models (~95k lines). Each runs its own strategy; they review each other's ideas and learn from failures. Nothing trades real money until it survives real costs.

- **Nemesis** — a falsification engine for Indian F&O: its job is to kill strategy ideas before they cost a rupee. 800+ tests, a real broker cost model, and a live options-chain recorder.
- **Ploutos** — a correctness-first research lab for NIFTY intraday ideas: causal simulator, pre-registered "alpha hunts", with results clearly labelled as research, not tradeable P&L.
- **NIAN** — an index-futures bot with long-horizon backtests, full cost modelling and a paper-trading mode.
- **Quasar** — multi-index trend ensemble with volatility-targeted, risk-parity position sizing.
- **Predator** — event-day options system: catalyst calendar → breakout signals → risk-governed paper trading loop.
- **TiltGuard** — a trading-discipline layer that sits between you and your broker: loss caps, revenge-trade cooldowns, position-size limits.
